Filming a room that scans well

  • Walk, don't spin. The app works out depth from the camera changing position. Turning on the spot gives it nothing, and is the most common reason a scan fails.
  • Go slowly, for 45 to 90 seconds. Circle the room facing inwards, then once more facing the walls. Fast moves blur the picture.
  • Turn every light on. Dark corners come out as fog.
  • Hold the phone sideways at chest height, and tilt it up and down now and then so floor and ceiling get seen.
  • Nothing should move. No people, pets or a TV playing.
  • Mirrors, windows and bare white walls are hard. They still appear, but expect them to look soft or odd.
